Sump Pump Installation & Repair

Dependable protection when the rain comes

A properly sized and maintained sump pump is essential for any basement waterproofing system in the Chester area. We install new pumps, upgrade existing systems, and add battery backup units so your home stays dry even during power outages.

Our Sump Pump Services

  • New primary sump pump installation
  • Battery backup and dual-pump systems
  • Pump replacement and repairs
  • Alarm systems and float switch upgrades
  • Annual maintenance and check-ups

Why Battery Backup Matters

Severe storms often cause power failures exactly when water tables rise. A battery backup pump continues operating so your basement does not flood. We recommend backup systems for most homes in our service area.

Whether you need a new installation or help with a failing pump, we respond quickly and install quality equipment built for Pennsylvania conditions.
